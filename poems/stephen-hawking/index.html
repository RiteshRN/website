RIP Stephen Hawking

2018 · IN MEMORIAM

If there is indeed a Book
With all the answers for those who look
A reward for meeting the suffering of life
At long last, solace from Uncertainty's knife
The nature of time and human intelligence
Revealed amidst tales of Universal adolescence

He is certainly reading it now
And he deserves this feeling of wow

A man cursed by a God who plays dice
A man for whom physics was paradise
A man who ventured far into paradox
A man who sought to live despite Death’s knocks
A man who fought to explain quantum gravity
A man who lived with gratitude and levity
A man who taught us all
Alas, he too must fall

RIP Stephen Hawking
